# Grantline feedback

Report bugs and request features for Grantline, a research grant budgeting and forecasting app.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/StefanKurtenbach/grantline-feedback/issues/new?labels=bug&title=Bug%3A%20)
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/StefanKurtenbach/grantline-feedback/issues/new?labels=enhancement&title=Feature%3A%20)

For bugs, include what happened, what you expected, steps to reproduce, and your browser or device.

Issues are public. Remove names, salaries, grant balances, email addresses, account identifiers, and confidential material from reports and screenshots.
